About

Understanding food and agriculture through economics and data

My research in agricultural and food economics focuses on food demand and consumer behavior, aggregation and price indices, and agricultural trade. Recently, I have also been building datasets on seafood distribution through AI-assisted transcription of historical Japanese documents and conducting research on the sensory evaluation of foods.
